# Community

## Ask and talk

[Join the Discord server](https://discord.gg/dfJUkmEtAP) for questions, ideas and help with your
setup. A message you don't expect is often already in [Troubleshooting](/guides/troubleshooting).

## Report a bug

[Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/lotun-io/vitest-cucumber/issues). It helps to include:

- the versions of `@lotun/vitest-cucumber`, Vitest, `@cucumber/cucumber` and Node.js (and Storybook,
  for a Storybook project);
- your Vitest config, or the part with `cucumber()`;
- the smallest feature file and step definitions that show it, and what Vitest printed.
